Shift Triage Privacy Policy

This app operates entirely locally. No user data, work schedules, calendar events, or financial information is ever collected, stored, transmitted, or shared with external servers.

The only time anything leaves your phone is when you choose to send a bug report or feature request by email. You see the message first and nothing is sent unless you press Send. Purchases are handled by Apple.
